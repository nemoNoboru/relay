Invitations, not accounts

Your people
vouch for
your people

There is no sign-up. An invitation link carries a key. Opening it makes the browser a member of this node. That is the whole account system: no email, no password, no recovery questions.

A member can invite someone they trust. The host can revoke a person and everyone they brought in with one action. The group grows by vouching, not by going viral.

Keep a node

One command.
Your own press.

An old laptop is enough. The node starts with the password relay; open the host console, change it, then make the first invitation.

curl -sSfL \
  https://raw.githubusercontent.com/nemoNoboru/relay/main/install.sh | sh

relay node --name my-group --data ~/relay --listen :8080
  1. Sign in at /_relay/admin.html with relay, then choose your own password.
  2. Make an invite for each person. They open the link, drop a page, and are live.
  3. They invite others in turn. Revoke one key and everyone below it is cut off too.
