Run a node for your people

Relay is a micro-social network you run yourselves.

One binary on a computer someone in the group keeps. Invitation links instead of accounts, no company in the middle.

curl -sSfL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/nemoNoboru/relay/main/install.sh | sh

relay node --name my-group --data ~/relay --listen :8080
  1. Sign in at /_relay/admin.html with relay, then choose your own password.
  2. Make an invite for each person. They open the link and are members.
  3. They share pages and invite others. Revoke one key and its branch is cut off.
