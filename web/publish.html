What is Relay?

A micro-social network for you and your real friends. The page you are on belongs to a server someone in the group keeps — a friend, a club, a reading group — not a company. Drop your files above and they are online. There is no account to make, nothing to install, and no bill at the end of the month.

Why a node instead of a platform?

Because the group should own the place. A node is one computer, one program, and one data folder, looked after by someone you can talk to. The pages are ordinary files at ordinary addresses. Nobody sits between you and your friends counting the visit.

The group is small on purpose: a handful of people, invited by each other. The plan is a quiet home where you see what your friends shared, in the order they shared it — no public feed to perform for and no algorithm deciding who gets seen.

What can I put here?

Photos, a link with a note, a zine, a page of writing, a poster for something happening on your street. If all you have is a single Markdown file, drop that: it is turned into a page for you. Each upload replaces the whole page, and the last ten versions are kept in case you want one back. The templates above make the common kinds of post easy.

Want one of these servers for your own group? Anyone can keep a node — it is one program on any computer, an old laptop included.

Invite someone

Your invitation is yours to pass on. Make a link for someone you trust and they get their own place in the group, on the same terms you did. Revoking you would cut them off too.

For AI assistants

Using Claude or another assistant? Copy this, paste it into the chat, and tell it where your page is.

You were sent a link to this page. The invitation key is the key value after # in that link; never print it or send it anywhere else. To publish, send a tar of the page to this server: 

tar -cf - -C PAGE_FOLDER . | curl --data-binary @- \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer KEY" \
  https://THIS-SERVER/_relay/v1/sites/PAGE_NAME/deploy

THIS-SERVER is the host of the link. Page names are lowercase letters, numbers and hyphens. The JSON reply's url field is the page's address. Each upload replaces the whole page. More detail: the publishing instructions.
