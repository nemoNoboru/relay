Publish a page

Step by step

A page is a file called index.html, plus any pictures or styles it uses. You need those, an invitation link from whoever keeps this server, and two minutes.

The easy way: open the invitation link you were sent and drop your page onto the publish page. No link? Open the publish page and paste your key. The steps below are for the terminal.

1. Open a terminal where your page is

  • Mac: right-click it in Finder, then New Terminal at Folder.
  • Windows: open it, click the address bar, type powershell and press Enter.
  • Linux: right-click inside it, then Open in Terminal.

2. Paste this line

Put your key in place of YOUR-KEY, and a name for your page in place of your-page: lowercase letters, numbers and hyphens. 

tar -cf - . | curl --data-binary @- \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer YOUR-KEY" \
  https://this-server/_relay/v1/sites/your-page/deploy

On Windows, paste these two lines instead:

tar -cf ..\page.tar .
curl.exe --data-binary "@..\page.tar" -H "Authorization: Bearer YOUR-KEY" https://this-server/_relay/v1/sites/your-page/deploy

3. Open your page

The reply ends with your page's address, after "url". Changed something? Paste the same line again. Every upload replaces the whole page, and the server keeps the last ten versions in case you need one back.

If it goes wrong

The reply saysWhat to do
bad_tokenThe key is wrong or was withdrawn. Ask for a new one.
bad_namePick a page name with only lowercase letters, numbers and hyphens.
bad_archiveSomething odd is in there: a shortcut or a link. Remove it and try again.
too_largeOver 256 MB. Make the images smaller.

Pages look broken? Write links as images/cat.jpg, not /images/cat.jpg: your page lives under a path on the server, not at the top.

Keep the key to yourself

Whoever holds the key is that member. Don't post it, don't put it in a shared file. If it leaks, ask the host to rotate it and the old one stops working.

Let an assistant do it

AI assistants can follow these instructions. Give them the server's address and your key, and nothing else.
