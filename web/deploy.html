Publish a site

Step by step

A website is just a folder with an index.html inside. You need that folder, a key from whoever keeps this server, and two minutes.

The easy way: open the invite link you were sent and drop your folder onto the page. No invite link? Open the publish page and paste your key. The steps below are for the terminal.

1. Open a terminal in your folder

  • Mac: right-click the folder in Finder, then New Terminal at Folder.
  • Windows: open the folder, click the address bar, type powershell and press Enter.
  • Linux: right-click inside the folder, then Open in Terminal.

2. Paste this line

Put your key in place of YOUR-KEY, and a name for your site in place of your-site: lowercase letters, numbers and hyphens. 

tar -cf - . | curl --data-binary @- \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer YOUR-KEY" \
  https://this-server/_relay/v1/sites/your-site/deploy

On Windows, paste these two lines instead:

tar -cf ..\site.tar .
curl.exe --data-binary "@..\site.tar" -H "Authorization: Bearer YOUR-KEY" https://this-server/_relay/v1/sites/your-site/deploy

3. Open your site

The reply ends with your site's address, after "url". Changed something? Paste the same line again. Every upload replaces the whole site, and the server keeps the last ten versions in case you need one back.

If it goes wrong

The reply saysWhat to do
bad_tokenThe key is wrong or was withdrawn. Ask for a new one.
bad_namePick a site name with only lowercase letters, numbers and hyphens.
bad_archiveSomething odd in the folder: a shortcut or a link. Remove it and try again.
too_largeOver 256 MB. Make the images smaller.

Pages look broken? Write links as images/cat.jpg, not /images/cat.jpg: your site lives in its own corner of the server, not at the top.

Keep the key to yourself

Whoever holds the key can change the site. Don't post it, don't put it in a shared file. If it leaks, ask for a new one and the old one stops working.

Let an assistant do it

AI assistants can follow these instructions. Give them the server's address and your key, and nothing else.
